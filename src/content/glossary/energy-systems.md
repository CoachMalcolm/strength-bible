- **Phosphagen:** all-out efforts up to about 10 seconds (a sprint, a max lift).
- **Glycolytic:** hard efforts from about 10 seconds to 2 minutes.
- **Aerobic:** anything longer, using oxygen.

All three are always running; the intensity decides which one leads. Good conditioning trains the systems your sport actually uses.
