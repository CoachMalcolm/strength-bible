term: "Metabolic Stress"
aliases:
  - "metabolic stress"

short: "The burn and pump from byproducts building up in a working muscle."
related_concepts:
  - "hypertrophy"
status: "draft"
---

During higher-rep sets, byproducts of energy production build up in the muscle. That's the burn you feel.

It's thought to contribute to muscle growth, but researchers see it as a smaller driver than mechanical tension.
