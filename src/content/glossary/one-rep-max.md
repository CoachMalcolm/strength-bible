Your 1RM is a snapshot of your max strength on a lift. Many programs set your working weights as a percentage of it, like 75% of 1RM.

You don't have to max out to know it. An **estimated 1RM (e1RM)** is calculated from a heavy set of several reps, which is safer and nearly as useful.
