category: "Measurement"
short: "A 1–10 score for how hard a set felt. 10 means you couldn't have done another rep."
related_concepts:
  - "autoregulation"
status: "draft"
---

RPE lets you match your training to how you actually feel that day. On a lifting scale, RPE 8 means you had about 2 reps left; RPE 10 means nothing left.

It takes practice to rate honestly. Logging RPE every session is how you get good at it.
