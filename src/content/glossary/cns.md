Strength is partly a skill your nervous system learns. Heavy lifting and all-out sprints are demanding on the CNS, which is one reason they need more rest than lighter work.

"CNS fatigue" gets blamed for a lot online; most tiredness after training has more ordinary causes like sleep and food.
