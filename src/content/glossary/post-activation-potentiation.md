---
term: "Potentiation"
aliases:
  - "post-activation potentiation"
  - "potentiation"
  - "PAP"
category: "Power & Speed"
short: "A short-term boost in power after a heavy or explosive effort."
related_concepts:
  - "power"
status: "draft"
---

After a heavy set or an explosive movement, your nervous system can stay "primed" for a few minutes, making the next explosive effort stronger.

Coaches use this by pairing a heavy lift with a jump. The effect varies a lot from person to person.
