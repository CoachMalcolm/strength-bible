## What we're building

A digital training companion that holds everything Coach Malcolm has learned in 40 years of training, coaching and competing — organized so anyone can learn it at their own pace and put it into practice.

It's for everyone: athletes, beginners, grandparents, serious lifters. Every idea is explained at four levels, from simple to scientific, with a quick check after each to make sure it clicked. It covers the body (strength, conditioning, nutrition) and the person (mindset, consistency, ownership, handling life while you train).

Down the road, coaches and programs will be able to use it with their own athletes.

## Where we are

**Working now on the live site:**
- **Lessons with checks.** Each concept has four levels. Answer the check right and the next level opens. Miss it and it explains the idea again a different way instead of just showing the answer.
- **Living glossary.** Technical words are underlined. Tap one to see what it means and mark "I know this" or "Still learning." A Glossary page keeps your personal list of what you know and what you're still learning.
- **Workout timer.** Build interval timers (rounds, rest, circuits) or use the ready-made ones (Tabata, EMOM, conditioning intervals, strength rest). Voice, beeps and vibration. Keeps exact time even if the phone locks.
  - **Mini mode:** shrink the timer to a small bubble you can drag anywhere and keep using the site.
  - **Pop out:** float the timer over other apps (texts, music) on phones that allow it.
  - **Share:** send a timer to an athlete as a link.

**Being prepared:**
- **Spanish** is the first additional language. A Spanish reviewer will check every translation before it goes live.
- **Coach Malcolm's own voice** for the timer, using voice-cloning (ElevenLabs). Malcolm is setting up the account and recording.
